Hello! I'm Sarah — a product leader with a finance and technology background, currently finishing up my PhD and actively looking for my next product role in fintech or tech.

Prior to my PhD, I spent 5+ years in capital markets and fintech: I led go-to-market for Morningstar Impact Solutions, launched Canada's first Carbon Credit ETF at Ninepoint Partners (CA$8B AUM), and covered infrastructure and real assets deals across Asia-Pacific at Macquarie (US$130B AUM). I am a CFA charterholder and hold the CFA Certificate in ESG Investing.

The deeper I got into finance and technology, the more questions I had — so I went back to school to answer them. I am completing my Ph.D. in Applied Science at Toronto Metropolitan University, where I design multi-factor models that integrate deep neural networks with traditional econometric methods to extract investment signals from unstructured financial, media, and alternative data. I also ended up enrolled in an MS in Computer Science at Georgia Tech — because I kept asking engineers "but how does it actually work?" and eventually decided to just find out myself. I also hold an MBA from Georgetown University (McDonough) and a BCom from UBC (Sauder). My work has appeared in leading management and finance outlets — including the Annual Meeting of the Academy of Management and Edward Elgar Publishing — with several papers currently under review.

I am fluent in English, Mandarin, and Python — currently based in San Francisco with no sponsorship required. Feel free to reach out for roles, collaborations, or just a chat about things — I'm much more fun in person!😄
