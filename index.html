Hello! I'm Sarah — a product leader with a finance and technology background, currently wrapping up my PhD and actively looking for my next product role in fintech or investment products.

Prior to my PhD, I spent 5+ years in capital markets and fintech: I led go-to-market for Morningstar Impact Solutions, launched Canada's first Carbon Credit ETF at Ninepoint Partners (CA$8B AUM), and covered infrastructure and real assets deals across Asia-Pacific at Macquarie (US$130B AUM). I am a CFA charterholder and hold the CFA Certificate in ESG Investing.

The deeper I got into finance and technology, the more questions I had — so I went back to school to answer them. I am completing my Ph.D. in Applied Science at Toronto Metropolitan University, where my research focuses on multi-factor models that integrate deep neural networks with traditional econometric methods to extract investment signals from unstructured financial, media, and alternative data. In parallel, I am pursuing an MS in Computer Science at Georgia Tech to strengthen the technical foundations underlying my work. My research has been published in leading academic and industry outlets, including the Academy of Management Annual Meeting and Edward Elgar Publishing, with additional papers under review.

I am fluent in English, Mandarin, and Python — currently based in San Francisco. Feel free to reach out for roles, collaborations, or just a chat about things — I'm much more fun in person!😄
